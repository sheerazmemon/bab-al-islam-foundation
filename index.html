About Us

A Foundation Built on Faith & Service

Bab Al Islam Foundation, meaning “Gateway to Islam,” is an organization committed to uplifting underserved communities across Pakistan by providing the essentials of dignified life: food, shelter, and clothing.

We believe every individual deserves dignity, security, and the basic necessities to live with honor. Our work is rooted in the Islamic principles of Zakaat (obligatory giving) and Sadaqah (voluntary charity), a reminder that our collective responsibility to one another is not optional, but a cornerstone of faith.
