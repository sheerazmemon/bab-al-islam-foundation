About Us

A Foundation Built on Faith & Service

Founded in 2005, Bab Al Islam Foundation — "The Door to Islam" — has served as a gateway of compassion, connecting underserved communities with the resources and support they need to thrive.

We believe every individual deserves dignity, access to knowledge, and the chance to reach their fullest potential. Our work is guided by the Islamic principles of sadaqah (charity), ta'awun (mutual cooperation), and 'adl (justice).

Our Programs & Services

From emergency relief to long-term education, our programs address the diverse needs of our community.

Islamic Education

Quranic studies, Arabic language classes, and Islamic studies programs for all age groups, including weekend and after-school options.

Learn more →

Food & Emergency Relief

Weekly food pantry, hot meal programs, and emergency financial assistance for families facing hardship in our community.

Learn more →

Youth Services

After-school tutoring, mentorship programs, leadership camps, and sports leagues that nurture the next generation of leaders.

Learn more →

Community Outreach

Interfaith dialogues, neighborhood clean-ups, health fairs, and partnerships with local organizations to strengthen social bonds.

Learn more →

Women Empowerment

Skill-building workshops, entrepreneurship support, counseling services, and a safe space for women to connect and grow.

Learn more →

New Muslim Support

Welcoming guidance, mentorship, and resources for those who have recently embraced Islam or are exploring the faith.
